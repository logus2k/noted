# Spain — Estrategia de Inteligencia Artificial 2024

**Source:** [spain_estrategia de inteligencia_artificial_2024.pdf](../../domains/national_ai_agendas/sources/spain_estrategia%20de%20inteligencia_artificial_2024.pdf), 67 pages (text extracted with `pdftotext`). Prólogo signed by **José Luis Escrivá**, Ministro para la Transformación Digital y de la Función Pública [p.5]. Published 2024. **Reinforcement and acceleration of the 2020 ENIA** (Estrategia Nacional de Inteligencia Artificial, published in 2020 under PRTR Hito 249), not a replacement [p.7].

## Document type

The 2024 strategy is a **reinforcement layer** on top of Spain's 2020 ENIA, not a clean-sheet rewrite — it consolidates what has been delivered under the Plan de Recuperación, Transformación y Resiliencia (PRTR) and **adds new initiatives in response to the generative-AI acceleration** of 2022-2024 [p.7]. This makes it the closest Iberian peer to Portugal's ANIA: same EU regulatory anchor, similar economy scale, parallel pillars, but with **two extra years of execution experience** to draw on.

## The two strategic centrepieces

### ALIA — Public Infrastructure for Foundation Language Models [Palanca 3, p.22-30]

Spain's single largest strategic bet:
- **Family of foundation models** in **castellano + 4 co-official languages** (catalán, euskera, gallego, valenciano), sizes 7B / 22B / 40B / 70B parameters, scaling to **175B** once MareNostrum 5 upgrade completes
- **Lifts Spanish weight in training data from typical <5% to >20%** [p.28]
- First version released **summer 2024** (multilingual base); instructed models Q4 2024; "Ibéricos" subfamily focused on **castellano + portugués + co-official languages** in 2025
- Distributed via HuggingFace / GitHub / Zenodo under permissive licenses where possible
- Specialised SLMs (Small Language Models) for biomedical, legal, then health/territory/justice/environment/tax [p.29]
- **15-signatory governance convenio** [p.26]: SEDIA, BSC-CNS (technical coordination), AESIA (validation), Instituto Cervantes, RAE, the academies of euskera/gallego/valencià + Institut d'Estudis Catalans, Ministerio de Cultura (with Biblioteca Nacional), Centro Nacional de Industrias del Español (La Rioja), and the ILENIA centres (HiTZ, ILG, CITIUS, CENID, CEATIC)
- Framed around **600 million Spanish-speakers worldwide** as the addressable market [p.5, p.22]

### AESIA — Agencia Española de Supervisión de la Inteligencia Artificial [Palanca 8, p.54-61]

- **First operational EU AI regulator** — created August 2023, anticipating EU AI Act enforcement [p.60]
- Headquartered in **A Coruña** (Galicia — deliberately not Madrid)
- Triple mandate:
  - **Think & Do Tank** [8.1] — generates national debate, sectoral foros, periodic reports, foresight on frontier AI risks (collaborates with UK + US AI safety institutes [p.59])
  - **Market Surveillance Authority + single point of contact** for EU AI Act high-risk system conformity assessment [8.2, p.60]
  - **AI Sandbox operator** + **international representation** in EU AI Committee, G20, OECD, UN High-Level Panel on AI, with explicit LATAM outreach via EU-LAC Digital Alliance [8.3, p.61]

## Notable mechanisms

1. **Per-Palanca named investment + per-Initiative deadline + per-Initiative funding source** [Table 6, p.64-65]. Tighter than any other strategy in this set, Portugal included.

2. **Programa Nacional de Algoritmos Verdes** — "Green AI by Design" with 4 axes: energy efficiency in model development, efficient infrastructure, AI+blockchain integration, Spanish-language Green AI market [p.32]. €3.24M small-budget but **named and structured**.

3. **Sustainable Data Centres pillar (Palanca 2)** — three integrated initiatives [p.18-21]:
   - 2.1: Transpose EU Directive 2023/1791 (>500 kW data centres must publish energy/renewables/water/demand; >1000 kW must have heat-recovery strategy)
   - 2.2: Sello + accelerated permits via single window + national CPD registry
   - 2.3: Voluntary "Sello de IA Sostenible" quality mark with maturity levels, tied to **EU AI Act mandatory documentation for GPAI energy consumption**

4. **Kit Consulting [Palanca 6.1, p.47]** — €300M, advisor-mediated (not platform-mediated) SME AI-adoption support, modeled on the proven Kit Digital. **Targets 15,000 pymes**. Available from May 2024.

5. **Sectoral nodes** under ALIA infrastructure [Palanca 6.3.2, p.49]: GovTech / Health-Biomedical / Education / Legal-Finance / Communication. Each node creates specialised models + corpus + sectoral collaborations.

6. **Próximo despliegue plan** — pre-commercial secure environment for SMEs + startups to do inference/fine-tuning/evaluation on ALIA models, distributed via RES nodes regionally [p.49].

7. **AESIA Sandbox as first deployment** — Spain anticipated EU AI Act sandboxes; the GovTech AGE pilots will be the **first cases** in the AESIA sandbox, generating implementation guides for the private sector [p.43].

8. **Programa "Desafíos Green AI"** — three categories (researchers / startups-scaleups / consolidated companies) competing on AI resource-efficiency [p.21].

9. **15 public universities** offering AI undergraduate + 20 master's programmes (2022-23); 5 private offerings; 3 specialized FP courses in IA / Big Data / Cybersecurity [p.35]. Quantified pipeline.

10. **CCAA (regional government) coordination** via Conferencia Sectorial para la Transformación Digital + FEDER funding €317M for RETECH territorial networks [p.32]. Spain explicitly addresses **regional AI capacity** — Portugal does not.

11. **EU-LAC outreach via ALIA** — explicit commitment to make Spanish-language AI models available to Latin American countries under the EU-LAC Digital Alliance [p.61]. Soft-power leverage of 600M speakers.

## What Spain does that Portugal's ANIA does NOT

1. **Aggregate budget transparency** — Spain publishes €600M deployed + ~€2B forward = **~€2.6B cumulative**. Portugal's ANIA aggregates nothing comparable; only specific lines (€100M BPF SMEs, €1.2B PA opportunity).

2. **Per-initiative funding source identification** (PRTR component number, FEDER, Next Tech). Portugal lists "Relevant Entities" but not the **funding line**.

3. **AESIA as operational regulator** — Portugal IV.4 ("Implementation of the EU AI Act") is still **planning** the "definition of competent authorities" [Portugal ANIA p.20]. Spain is **2+ years ahead** institutionally.

4. **ALIA-scale sovereign language model commitment.** Portugal's AMALIA (II.7) is framed as "**continuation of the project, expanding to new use cases**" [Portugal ANIA p.14] — a continuation, not a strategic centerpiece. Spain frames ALIA as **infrastructure** and assembles a 15-signatory consortium around it. Portuguese-language sovereignty deserves equivalent treatment given Portugal anchors the CPLP (~260M speakers).

5. **Linguistic-export strategy** to a hispanophone (or in Portugal's case lusophone) world. Spain's EU-LAC offer of ALIA [p.61] is the explicit instrument. Portugal has no equivalent CPLP-AI strategy in ANIA.

6. **Green AI / Algoritmos Verdes** as a named programme. Portugal has nothing parallel — renewables are mentioned as an asset [Portugal ANIA p.4] but not connected to AI compute or model training.

7. **Sustainable Data Centres as a full Palanca** with three integrated initiatives. Portugal I.4 (National Data Centre Plan) is one bullet.

8. **Operational AI Sandbox** at AESIA. Portugal IV.5 is "to be defined" [Portugal ANIA p.21].

9. **Quantum Spain programme** — explicit €22M, first southern-European quantum computer. Portugal ANIA names quantum in passing.

10. **CCAA / regional coordination** mechanism via Conferencia Sectorial. Portugal does not address regional/island disparity in AI capacity.

11. **Explicit kit-consulting (€300M, 15,000 pymes target)** — sized, dated, with implementation start date. Portugal II.9 + II.10 are smaller and less specific.

12. **Quantified educational pipeline** — 15 + 20 + 5 + 3 university and FP AI programmes. Portugal lists initiatives without enumerating current pipeline state.

13. **Governance structure with annual reporting to Consejo de Ministros** [p.62] + dedicated Comisión Interministerial under SEDIA + Comisión de Estrategia TIC semestral meetings + Conferencia Sectorial CCAA. Portugal scatters across entities without a clear cadence.

## What Portugal does that Spain's strategy does NOT

1. **Quantitative GDP impact target** — Portugal €18-22B/yr GDP add, +2.7pp productivity contribution, 8% GDP at peak [Portugal ANIA p.3]. Spain has no equivalent macro figure (only Spain-vs-EU productivity comparison).

2. **Six guiding principles** [Portugal ANIA p.6] — clean conceptual scaffolding. Spain has 3 ejes but no equivalent set of cross-cutting principles.

3. **Hybrid Development/Application positioning** [Portugal ANIA p.7] — explicit strategic-positioning debate. Spain implicitly does both via ALIA + Kit Consulting but doesn't frame the choice.

4. **AI Fast Track talent visa** (III.5) [Portugal ANIA p.18] — Spain has Spain Talent Hub (broader, €18M) but no specific AI visa instrument.

5. **AI literacy citizen campaign (III.8)** + **AI Generation career campaign (III.7)** [Portugal ANIA p.18] — Portugal has dedicated public-awareness initiatives. Spain's talent track is heavier on professional skills training, lighter on citizen-facing awareness.

6. **PA Procurement Code Practical Guide (II.13)** [Portugal ANIA p.15] — Spain has Compra Pública Innovadora (€317M RETECH bundle) but no equivalent procurement-interpretation guide.

## Critique-lens conclusion

Spain is **the most directly importable peer to Portugal's ANIA in this set** — same EU framework, Iberian peninsula geography, similar economy scale, parallel pillar structures, and Portugal even appears as a target language in ALIA's "Ibéricos" subfamily [p.28]. The 2024 Spanish strategy was launched two years before Portugal's ANIA and shows what Portugal could look like with two more years of execution.

**Top moves Portugal should import:**

1. **Upgrade AMALIA to ALIA-scale ambition.** Portugal's II.7 frames AMALIA as a "continuation" [Portugal ANIA p.14]. Match Spain's commitment: a multi-billion-parameter foundation-model family in **Portuguese + the Lusophone variants**, with a **CPLP-wide consortium** (Brazil, Angola, Mozambique, Cabo Verde, Guinea-Bissau, São Tomé and Príncipe, East Timor — 260M speakers). The model becomes Portugal's soft-power export to lusophone Latin America/Africa. Spain's EU-LAC strategy [p.61] is the explicit template.

2. **Upgrade IV.3 (Center for Responsible AI) to a full AESIA-equivalent regulator.** AESIA was operational from August 2023 and is now the EU's reference implementation. Portugal IV.4 says "definition of competent authorities" is still pending [Portugal ANIA p.20] — this institutional gap is **the largest comparative weakness** in ANIA against Spain. The Centro para a IA Responsável should be the seed of a Portuguese-flagged regulator with sandbox + market-surveillance + international-representation functions.

3. **Publish per-initiative funding source + aggregate budget.** Spain's Tabla 6 is the gold standard in this comparison set. Portugal should add a budget annex to ANIA naming the funding line (PRR Component, FEDER, BPF, BfP, IAPMEI, PFA, …) for each of the 32 initiatives.

4. **Add a Sustainable Data Centres Palanca / Pillar.** Portugal claims renewable energy as advantage [Portugal ANIA p.4] but never operationalises it. Spain's Palanca 2 [p.18-21] is the import template: (a) transpose Directive 2023/1791 ahead of deadline, (b) sello + accelerated permits + national CPD registry, (c) voluntary AI-sustainability quality mark.

5. **Add a Programa Nacional de Algoritmos Verdes** — Green AI by Design [p.32]. Small budget (€3.24M) but **named programme** with 4 axes. Portugal can copy this nearly verbatim.

6. **Operationalise an AI Sandbox under IV.5 immediately** rather than as a future definition. Spain has been running its sandbox since AESIA's creation; Portugal can leverage Spain's already-published guides and Iberian collaboration.

7. **Adopt a Kit-Consulting analogue** for the SME track. Portugal II.9 + II.10 (BFP credit line + PME.IA platform) cover financing and tools but not advisory. Spain's Kit Consulting (€300M, 15,000 pymes, May 2024 start) is a sized, dated, advisor-mediated complement.

8. **Sectoral nodes structure** under II.5 Sectoral AI Centres — explicit hubs for GovTech, Health-Biomedical, Education, Legal, Communication. Spain's Palanca 6.3.2 is the template [p.49].

9. **Quantum strategy** — Portugal mentions quantum in passing. Spain has a €22M Quantum Spain programme. Even a small Portuguese quantum-AI bridge (linked to the existing Iberian Nanotechnology Laboratory in Braga, or via the Atlantic Center for Quantum) would strengthen ANIA.

10. **Regional / island coordination mechanism.** Portugal has Madeira and Azores autonomous regions, plus the interior-coast divide. Spain's Conferencia Sectorial CCAA model is directly importable as a Portuguese Conferência Nacional de Transformação Digital.

**What Spain can borrow from Portugal:**
- The clean six-guiding-principles framing [Portugal ANIA p.6]
- The explicit quantitative GDP / productivity case [p.3]
- The AI Fast Track visa instrument (III.5)
- The Doutor AP civil-service training programme (III.1)
- The AI literacy citizen campaign (III.8)
- The hybrid Development/Application strategic-positioning debate [p.7]

The two countries should formally coordinate — Spain's ALIA "Ibéricos" subfamily is already designed for shared use, and a Portugal-Spain joint Iberian AI strategy would be more competitive globally than either country alone.
