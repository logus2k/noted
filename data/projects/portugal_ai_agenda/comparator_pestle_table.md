## At-a-glance horizontal patterns (per Portuguese-relevant peer cluster)

**Iberian + Romance language cluster (PT, ES, IT, FR, BR):** All EU AI Act anchored except BR (Bill 2338/23 pending). All have named or planned sovereign LLM strategies (AMALIA / ALIA / LMM / Mistral / PT-BR LLM Action 9). ES has the strongest budget granularity (Tabla 6) and BR matches with R$ 23B 5-axis breakdown — both are templates for the Portugal budget-granularity gap.

**Multi-doc consolidated peer cluster (PT, UK, AU, DE, SE, JP):** Portugal is moving toward multi-doc (ANIA + EDN + PIAAP planned). UK (Action Plan + Playbook), AU (3-doc), DE (Strategy + Aktionsplan), SE (Strategy + Action Plan), JP (Strategy 2022 + Basic Plan 2025) all show that multi-doc stacks are normal for serious AI commitments — Portugal's PIAAP would put it in this cluster naturally.

**Crisis-resilience cluster (JP + AU + PT-potential):** JP is the only jurisdiction with an explicit Strategic Objective 0 (National + Planetary Resilience); AU has Indigenous Data Sovereignty + climate-tech; PT could naturally join via wildfire + Atlantic seismic + Açores volcanism + climate (synthesis R26).

**Renewable-energy-advantage cluster (BR 89%, FI 75%+, PT 60%, SE 60%+, ES 50%, DE 50%):** Portugal sits mid-cluster with above-EU-average renewable share. BR is the global leader. Strategy implication: Portugal can credibly position as a **green data-centre destination** (synthesis R1 + R27 Digital Twin Cidades 129).

**No-dedicated-national-AI-Act cluster (everyone except JP):** Japan is uniquely positioned with Act No. 53/2025. All EU jurisdictions rely on EU AI Act + national transposition; UK has stalled future legislation; US uses executive orders; BR's Bill 2338/23 is pending. Portugal joining the "national AI Act" cluster (synthesis R22) would be a strong differentiator.
