## Document character

**ANIA** is the **national AI strategy of the XXV Constitutional Government**, published January 2026, approved by **Resolução do Conselho de Ministros (RCM) n.º 2/2026**. It sits inside the broader **Estratégia Digital Nacional (EDN) Plano de Ação 2026-2027** as Ação 20 (of 20). The architecture is therefore a two-tier stack: EDN at the umbrella digital-transformation level (covering State Reform Tecnológica + Data + Public Services + Economy + Digital Skills + AI), and ANIA at the AI-specific level (operationalising Ação 20 through 32 named initiatives). Compared to peers, this is structurally most similar to **Japan's pre-statutory AI Strategy 2022** in Cabinet Office framing, though without the subsequent statutory anchor (Japan moved to AI Act No. 53/2025 + Basic Plan; Portugal has the EU AI Act + RCM 2/2026 but no dedicated national AI Act — see synthesis R22).

## Vision and quantitative anchors

**Vision target [ANIA p.5]:** "Use AI as a driver of competitiveness at the service of society, propelling Portugal toward the European forefront and ensuring that the use of technology translates into economic opportunity, productivity, and public value."

**Headline quantitative claims [ANIA p.2, p.3]:**

| Metric | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Portugal productivity per hour worked vs EU average | **75%** | Starting baseline |
| AI productivity contribution to GDP growth (rapid-adoption scenario) | **+2.7 percentage points** over the next decade | McKinsey-cited |
| GDP add per year at peak impact | **€18-22 billion (~8% of GDP)** | Single largest aggregate macroeconomic claim in the comparator set |
| Productivity gap with EU (2010-22 historical) | PT 0.6 pp/yr vs EU 1.3 pp/yr vs US 2.1 pp/yr | The gap AI is meant to close |
| EuroHPC MareNostrum5 quota for PT research | ~5% (research) + 8-9% (innovation) | Explicitly flagged in ANIA as insufficient |

**Competitive advantages enumerated [ANIA p.4]:** fibre-optic 3rd in EU (92% homes); submarine-cable hub (only EU country with direct cable to South America); 3rd in EU by engineering students share + 9.6 researchers per 1,000 workers; **63% energy from renewables** (vs EU 45% average); 7th safest country globally + 6th for quality of life; **90% of Portuguese believe AI makes their lives easier**; 5,091 startups + 6 of 7 national unicorns AI-focused; 3 sovereign rating upgrades in 2025.

## Structure — 4 Eixos × 32 initiatives × 6 guiding principles

### The 4 Eixos (Areas of Action) [ANIA p.9]

| Eixo | Title | Initiatives | Lead concept |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I** | Infraestrutura e Dados | 4 (I.1-I.4) | Computational capacity + robust data economy; reduce external dependence |
| **II** | Inovação e Adoção | 13 (II.1-II.13) | Protect fundamental research + accelerate adoption across economy + PA + SMEs |
| **III** | Talento e Competências | 8 (III.1-III.8) | Train + attract + mobilise + retain talent for national competitiveness |
| **IV** | Responsabilidade e Ética | 7 (IV.1-IV.7) | Responsible R&D ecosystem + effective and efficient regulatory regime |

### The 32 initiatives (named, with start-date + lead entity per ANIA p.11-21)

**Eixo I — Infrastructure and Data (4):**
- **I.1** Advanced Computing and AI Factory (EuroHPC) — Deucalion expansion + EuroHPC network alignment (lead FCT; start 2S 2025)
- **I.2** Establish a Gigafactory in Portugal (lead BFP + AICEP; start 2S 2025)
- **I.3** Creating data spaces in critical areas (health, education, industry, PA — aligned with European Data Spaces / DEP) (lead ARTE + ANI + PA Entities; start 1S 2026)
- **I.4** National Data Centre Plan (lead Portuguese Government; start 2S 2025)

**Eixo II — Innovation and Adoption (13):**
- *Protect fundamental AI research:* **II.1** Fundamental research projects; **II.2** International networks + attract research centres
- *Connect research with companies + PA:* **II.3** Incentives for AI research in companies (IFIC + SIFIDE); **II.4** Non-academic PhDs + applied research; **II.5** Sectoral AI Centres (health/education/industrial); **II.6** "Opportunities in AI" national platform; **II.7** **AMALIA** continuation + new use cases; **II.8** Review of AI IP and patents regime
- *Increase AI adoption in SMEs:* **II.9** AI in SMEs (IFIC mechanisms); **II.10** "PME.IA" — low-code/no-code platform
- *Increase AI adoption in PA:* **II.11** AI Centre of Excellence in PA (lead ARTE); **II.12** National AI Calls for PA; **II.13** Practical CCP Guide for AI procurement

**Eixo III — Talent and Skills (8):**
- *Accelerate workforce skills:* **III.1** Accelerated AI training plan in PA (Doutor AP); **III.2** National Smart Skills Framework; **III.3** Micro-credentials + CTESP
- *International mobility:* **III.4** goPortugal expansion (MIT / UT Austin / Carnegie Mellon partnerships); **III.5** **AI Fast Track visa**
- *Talent engagement:* **III.6** National AI Week; **III.7** "AI Generation" campaign for young people; **III.8** AI literacy action for citizens (ANACOM + CNCS + IPQ + ARTE)

**Eixo IV — Responsibility and Ethics (7):**
- *Responsible AI R&D ecosystem:* **IV.1** Incentives for Responsible AI Research (including AI Economics and Work Impact, Algorithm Auditing); **IV.2** Startup acceleration in responsible AI (with NP 4595 standard); **IV.3** Continuity of the **Centre for Responsible AI**
- *Regulatory framework:* **IV.4** Implementation of the EU AI Act (define competent authorities, coordination model, sanctioning framework); **IV.5** Regulatory sandboxes; **IV.6** EU AI Act implementation guide + standards + risk-assessment tools; **IV.7** International cooperation and technology diplomacy

### The 6 guiding principles [ANIA p.6]

1. **Responsible Innovation** — ethics, security, transparency, privacy across the agenda
2. **Focus on Strategic Bets** — concentrate resources on high-transformation areas
3. **Public Administration as a Catalyst** — PA must lead by example
4. **More than Technology** — 70% organisational + human, 20% data/infrastructure, 10% technology
5. **Product-driven AI** — concrete, useful, scalable use cases
6. **Build on what already works** — continuous evaluation, prevent redundancy

## Distinctive Portuguese mechanisms

### 1. AMALIA — sovereign Portuguese LLM (PT-PT) [II.7]

The single named flagship initiative. PT-PT specifically (separate from Brazil's PBIA Action 9 PT-BR — see [feedback_pt_pt_pt_br_distinct_llms]). Lead: ARTE + FCT. Continuation phase expands use cases and evaluates international + public-private partnerships. **Direct peer:** Spain's ALIA (the closest Iberian counterpart; potential "Ibéricos" subfamily collaboration).

### 2. AI Fast Track visa [III.5]

Accelerated visa regime specifically for highly-qualified AI researchers and professionals. Lead: AIMA. **Stronger than most EU peers** which rely on generic EU Blue Card; closer to UK's High Potential Individual (HPI) visa or Singapore's Tech.Pass.

### 3. ARTE — Agência para a Reforma Tecnológica do Estado

Established by **Decreto-Lei n.º 96/2025**, headed by the **CTO do Estado**. Lead entity on ≥12 of the 20 EDN Ações + houses the PAANIA II.11 AI Centre of Excellence in PA. ARTE is the operational backbone of both EDN and ANIA's PA-AI thread. **No exact comparator analogue** — partially similar to UK's GDS (Government Digital Service) but with explicit AI mandate, or Singapore's GovTech.

### 4. Centre for Responsible AI (CIR) [IV.3]

National coordination body for AI ethics + responsibility (ecosystem disinemation + impact assessment + good-practice dissemination). Continuation under ANIA. **Direct peer:** UK AI Safety Institute / Japan AISI (synthesis R-implication: upgrade CIR to AISI-equivalent scale; see R-discussion in [portugal_ania_synthesis.md](portugal_ania_synthesis.md)).

### 5. Doutor AP — Accelerated PA-AI training [III.1]

Advanced training programme for civil servants — leaders + critical-process operators + new work models. Lead: ARTE + INA. **Distinctive in being centrally-named** vs most peers' generic civil-service training language; synthesis R16 proposes extending from advanced cohort to universal civil-servant mandate.

### 6. Six guiding principles as compact named enumeration [ANIA p.6]

Portugal's principles set is more aphoristic and citable than most peers' implicit principle statements. Principle (3) "Public Administration as a Catalyst" is particularly distinctive — it elevates PA-AI rhetorically (though structurally PA is distributed across three Eixos, not consolidated — see synthesis Pillar 4 framing).

### 7. EDN umbrella with named cross-references [EDN PA 2026-27 throughout]

Every EDN Ação carries a "Enquadramento EDN" tag pointing to the parent EDN Iniciativa. This dual-layer reference architecture (Iniciativas → Ações) is more explicit than most peer national strategies. ANIA = Ação 20, anchored to EDN Iniciativa #10 (Agenda Nacional de Inteligência Artificial).

### 8. CPLP positioning [implicit in IV.7; EDN Ação 2.5 explicit]

EDN Ação 2.5 explicitly names parcerias internacionais with **"países de língua oficial portuguesa"** — direct anchor for CPLP-scale AI cooperation. Portugal's natural diplomatic surface for a CPLP AI Framework (synthesis R23).

### 9. Atlantic submarine-cable hub [ANIA p.4; EDN Ação 13.4]

Only EU country with direct submarine cable to South America. EDN Ação 13.4 commits to the new **Anel CAM (Continente-Açores-Madeira)** + **Anel Açores** submarine-cable systems. Physical-layer foundation for an Atlantic-Lusophone Data Space (synthesis R1).

### 10. 63% renewable energy share with explicit AI infrastructure framing [ANIA p.4 + EDN Ação 13.2]

Above-EU-average renewable share (vs 45% EU average) coupled with the **Plano Nacional de Centros de Dados** (EDN Ação 13.2). Distinguishes Portugal from gas-dominant or coal-dominant peers; supports the synthesis-level "Watt-Bit Atlantic" framing (analogue of Japan's Watt-Bit Collaboration).

### 11. AI Continent + Apply AI alignment [ANIA Executive Summary p.2]

Portugal explicitly aligns ANIA with the EU **AI Continent Action Plan** and the **Apply AI** strategy. Stronger EU-alignment framing than UK (anti-EU AI Act stance) or AU (sector-based outside EU). Direct contrast with Japan's autonomous AI Act path.

### 12. Single-document parsimony — 22 pages

Portugal's strategy is significantly more compact than most peers (Brazil 66pp; UK Playbook 118pp; Australia 3-document stack ~90pp; Germany 2-document stack). Easier for stakeholder communication; the trade-off is reduced operational granularity (no per-initiative budget figures or quantified KPIs).

## What Portugal does that international peers do NOT (or does distinctively)

- **EDN umbrella with explicit AI-specific sub-strategy positioned as a numbered Ação** — most peers either have AI as standalone (US, UK, JP) or fold into broader digital strategy without explicit numbering. The EDN Ação 20 framing is a hybrid that supports clean cross-referencing.
- **AMALIA as named sovereign LLM with explicit PT-PT vs PT-BR distinction** — direct response to the Brazil PBIA Action 9 PT-BR programme; no other comparator pair has this kind of language-variant coordination opportunity at sovereign-LLM level.
- **6 guiding principles + named "PA as a Catalyst" principle** — the rhetorical elevation of PA-AI is structurally distinctive even though operationally PA is distributed across three Eixos.
- **AI Fast Track visa as a named instrument** [III.5] — most peers have generic talent attraction language without naming a dedicated visa stream.
- **Atlantic-Lusophone diplomatic positioning** — naturally distinctive given Portugal's geography + CPLP membership; no other small-state has the same "EU-side hub for Lusophone data sovereignty" potential.
- **63% renewable energy share with AI-infrastructure framing** — distinct competitive edge vs gas-dominant / coal-dominant peers.
- **Single-document parsimony at 22 pages** — easier stakeholder communication than the 66pp+ peer norm.

## Critique-lens conclusion

Portugal's ANIA is **a coherent, EU-anchored, compactly-articulated national AI strategy** with strong infrastructure foundations (Eixo I), a named sovereign LLM ambition (AMALIA), and explicit alignment to broader EU AI policy (AI Act + AI Continent + Apply AI). Its principal structural distinctiveness vs peers is the **dual-layer EDN-umbrella + ANIA-detail architecture** (Ação 20 framing).

Its two structural gaps vs the 16-comparator framework are **(a) PA-AI distributed across three Eixos rather than consolidated as a standalone pillar** (addressed by the PIAAP draft as the consolidation vehicle), and **(b) Society folded into the 6 guiding principles rather than treated as a standalone pillar** (addressed by Option B in the synthesis — named Society cross-cutting subsection within Eixo IV).

Beyond the structural gaps, **28 specific delivery refinements (R1-R28)** are catalogued in the [synthesis](portugal_ania_synthesis.md), drawing on the closest 4 peers: **Spain** (Iberian + Romance LLM), **Italy** (Mediterranean industrial twin), **Japan** (demographic + trustworthiness + Strategic Objective 0 + Digital Twins), **Singapore** (small-state ambitious + AI Verify directly importable). The single highest-leverage import is the **PIAAP** (R12) consolidating ANIA's distributed PA-AI thread; the second is the **Portuguese AI Law** (R22, Japan-template).
