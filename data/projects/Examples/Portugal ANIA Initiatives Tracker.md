### I. Infrastructure and Data
*   **Focus:** Developing strategic computing capacity and building a robust data economy while actively reducing external dependence [markdown_chunk:9d0c0a454d70].
*   **🇵🇹 Portugal ANIA: Infrastructure and Data**
    *   **Strategic Objectives:**
        *   **Hybrid Positioning:** The strategy is designed to be hybrid, maximizing the **Application** role (using existing AI for immediate productivity gains) to finance the **Development** role (foundational research for long-term, proprietary, and sovereign advantage) [markdown_chunk:0ca09c881869].
        *   **Development Focus:** Strategic investment in the Development role is essential for structural growth and generating high-leverage productivity gains through creating intellectual property and new products [markdown_chunk:0ca09c881869].

        *   **Computing Deficit:** Portugal faces a significant limitation in computing infrastructure and data management, with demand growing rapidly (41% per year) while supply is limited [markdown_chunk:bf2197740cd2].
        *   **Data Economy Barriers:** The national data economy is hampered by fragmented, inaccessible, and underused data due to bureaucratic, legal, and technical barriers [markdown_chunk:bf2197740cd2].
        *   **Adoption vs. Capability:** Current capabilities are primarily geared toward research and early-stage development, raising the risk of dependence on foreign infrastructure if internal capabilities are not strengthened [markdown_chunk:bf2197740cd2].

*   **🇵🇹 Portugal ANIA: Innovation and Adoption**
    *   **Objectives:** The primary goal is to accelerate AI adoption in Portugal, using the Public Administration as a key catalyst to transform knowledge into economic and public impact [markdown_chunk:8e41f490f319].

### III. Talent and Skills
*   **Focus:** Training, attracting, and retaining AI talent on a scale necessary to sustain national competitiveness [markdown_chunk:9d0c0a454d70].
*   **🇵🇹 Portugal ANIA: Talent and Skills**
    *   **Objectives:** The ANIA aims to position AI as an engine of professional development, promoting continuous training and better salaries, and ensuring positive societal impact [markdown_chunk:887f204f05ea, markdown_chunk:229e2113489a].
    *   **Current Challenges & Gaps:**
        *   **Talent Shortage:** The specialized talent base is limited, with only 11% of professionals reporting advanced skills in AI [markdown_chunk:229e2113489a].
        *   **Recruitment Difficulty:** Despite growing demand (over 20% per year), 32% of organizations face recruitment difficulties [markdown_chunk:229e2113489a].
        *   **CEO Perception:** The shortage of technical skills is cited by 50% of Portuguese CEOs as the main barrier to AI adoption [markdown_chunk:229e2113489a].
        *   **Structural Change:** The labour market requires the reskilling of around 1.3 million jobs and the potential displacement of 320,000 workers by 2030 [markdown_chunk:229e2113489a].
