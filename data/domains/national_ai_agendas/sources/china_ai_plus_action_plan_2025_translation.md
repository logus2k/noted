## State Council Opinion on Deeply Implementing the "AI+" Action

Guo Fa [2025] No. 11

To all Provincial People's Governments, Autonomous Regions, Direct-controlled Municipalities, and all Ministries and Directly Affiliated Institutions under the State Council:

To deeply implement the "AI+" action, promote extensive and in-depth integration of artificial intelligence across economic and social sectors and industries, reshape human production and lifestyle patterns, and foster revolutionary leaps in productivity and profound changes in production relations, the following opinion is hereby presented.

## I. Overall Requirements

Guided by Xi Jinping Thought on Socialism with Chinese Characteristics for a New Era, fully and accurately implementing the new development philosophy, and adhering to a people-centered development approach, leveraging our nation's abundant data resources, complete industrial systems, and expansive application scenarios, we shall reinforce forward-looking planning, systematic deployment, industry-specific strategies, open collaboration, and controllable security. Focusing on science and technology, industry, consumption, welfare, governance, and global cooperation, we shall deeply implement the "AI+" action, generating advances in new infrastructure, technology systems, industrial ecosystems, and employment opportunities, accelerating cultivation of new productive forces and ensuring all people benefit from AI development.

**Targets:**

**By 2027** — Achieve widespread deep integration of AI with six priority sectors; new-generation smart terminals and intelligent entities attain adoption rates exceeding **70%**; intelligent economy core industries expand rapidly; AI strengthens public governance; open cooperation frameworks continue improving.

**By 2030** — AI comprehensively empowers high-quality development; smart terminal and intelligent entity adoption exceeds **90%**; intelligent economy becomes a significant growth driver; technology democratization advances.

**By 2035** — Enter a new phase of intelligent economy and society development, providing strong support for achieving basic socialist modernization.

## II. Implementing Priority Actions

### (I) "AI+" Science and Technology

**1. Accelerate Scientific Discovery**

Explore new research paradigms driven by artificial intelligence, accelerating "zero-to-one" breakthrough discoveries. Construct and apply scientific large models; upgrade fundamental research platforms and major technological infrastructure; establish high-quality open scientific datasets; enhance "multi-modal complex scientific data processing" capabilities. Strengthen AI's cross-disciplinary integration role.

**2. Drive Innovation in Technology Research and Development**

Promote integrated coordination among AI-driven research, engineering implementation, and product commercialization, accelerating technology commercialization and iterative breakthroughs. Support intelligent R&D tools; strengthen collaboration between AI and biotechnology, quantum science, and sixth-generation mobile communications (6G). Link scientific achievements to application scenarios; connect application demands to technological innovation.

**3. Innovate Philosophy and Social Science Research Methods**

Transition research methodologies toward human-machine collaboration; explore new organizational forms; expand research perspectives. Study AI's profound effects on human cognition and ethical frameworks, developing theoretical systems for beneficial AI ("智向善" / smart-for-good).

### (II) "AI+" Industrial Development

**1. Cultivate Intelligent Native New Models and Industries**

Encourage enterprises integrating AI into strategy, organizational structure, and business processes; promote industry-wide intelligent transformation; develop new strategic emerging and future industry pathways. Develop intelligent native technologies and services; cultivate enterprises with AI-based foundational architectures; explore novel business models.

**2. Advance Industry-Wide Intelligent Development**

Drive intelligent coordination across industrial elements; accelerate AI application throughout design, pilot, production, service, and operations. Elevate workforce AI literacy; develop reusable expert knowledge; innovate industrial software; advance smart manufacturing equipment; enhance supply chain coordination; optimize production processes; deepen industrial internet integration.

**3. Accelerate Agricultural Digital-Smart Transformation**

Innovate AI-driven breeding systems; support agricultural smart applications in cultivation and breeding. Develop intelligent agricultural machinery, drones, and robots; enhance production tool capabilities; strengthen platform-based, intelligent equipment management. Apply AI to production management and risk prevention.

**4. Innovate Service Industry Development Models**

Transition from internet-enabled digital services toward "AI-driven service" approaches; expand service coverage; advance modernization. Explore hybrid human-AI service models. Promote new-generation smart terminals and intelligent entities across software, information, finance, commerce, legal, transportation, logistics, and retail sectors.

### (III) "AI+" Consumer Quality Enhancement

**1. Expand Service Consumption Scenarios**

Cultivate expansive, enriched intelligent service industries; develop efficiency-oriented and companion-type native applications; support smart assistants; strengthen consumption infrastructure; enhance lifestyle services; expand experience, personalized, cognitive, and emotional consumption scenarios.

**2. Cultivate Product Consumption New Industries**

Promote "ubiquitous intelligence" across terminals; develop smart product ecosystems; advance intelligent connected vehicles, AI smartphones and computers, robots, smart homes, wearables. Integrate AI with metaverse, low-altitude flight, additive manufacturing, brain-computer interfaces; explore novel smart product forms.

### (IV) "AI+" Public Welfare

**1. Create More Intelligent Working Methods**

Leverage AI for job creation and traditional role empowerment; explore human-machine collaborative organizational structures; cultivate intelligent agent work forms; support high-risk, labor-shortage positions. Advance AI skills training; promote innovation, entrepreneurship, and reemployment. Assess employment risks; guide resources toward job-creation potential; mitigate employment disruption.

**2. Implement More Effective Learning Methods**

Integrate AI throughout educational elements and processes; innovate human-machine collaborative teaching models; shift from knowledge transmission toward capability development; achieve large-scale personalized instruction; enhance quality and equity. Construct intelligent interactive learning scenarios; enable flexible, resource-rich self-directed learning.

**3. Build Higher-Quality Better Living**

Explore accessible health assistants; advance AI applications in diagnostic support, health management, healthcare insurance. Promote AI in cultural production, transmission, and exchange; create culturally-rooted content; expand cultural industries. Strengthen human relationships, emotional support, eldercare, childcare, disability assistance, and fitness through AI; extend AI applications throughout housing lifecycles; construct warmer intelligent society.

### (V) "AI+" Governance Capacity

**1. Pioneer Social Governance Human-Machine Coexistence**

Intelligently upgrade municipal infrastructure; explore smart city planning aligned with next-generation terminals; enhance urban operations. Extend AI to rural areas; advance urban-rural intelligent equalization. Conduct social AI experiments. Prudently advance government AI applications; create demand-responsive, proactive, intelligent government services. Accelerate AI deployment in public procurement; enhance intelligent transaction and oversight.

**2. Establish Diverse Multi-stakeholder Public Safety Governance**

Construct integrated governance systems addressing natural persons, digital entities, and intelligent robots; strengthen AI application in safety production, disaster prevention, threat prediction, social order. Enhance monitoring, enforcement, command, rescue, mobilization. Strengthen national security through AI; advance network space governance through intelligent threat identification, situational assessment, risk response.

**3. Advance Ecological Governance for Beautiful China**

Enhance integrated sky-ground-sea dynamic perception; strengthen intelligent territorial planning; optimize resource allocation. Strengthen AI monitoring, prediction, simulation capabilities addressing atmosphere, water, ocean, soil, biology, carbon markets; establish intelligent collaborative precision governance models.

### (VI) "AI+" Global Cooperation

**1. Promote AI Inclusive Sharing**

Position AI as international public good; establish equitable, trustworthy, diverse, mutually-beneficial AI ecosystem. Deepen high-level AI openness; advance technology open-sourcing; strengthen international collaboration in computing power, data, talent; support Global South capacity-building; ensure equitable participation; bridge global AI divides.

**2. Co-construct Global AI Governance Framework**

Support United Nations leadership in AI governance; explore frameworks enabling broad participation; collectively address global challenges. Deepen exchanges with international organizations; align governance rules and technical standards; jointly assess risks and ensure safe, reliable, controllable development.

## III. Strengthen Foundational Support Capacity

### (VII) Enhance Model Foundation Capabilities

Strengthen fundamental AI research; support diverse technological exploration and architecture innovation. Advance efficient training and inference methods; coordinate theoretical, technical, and engineering innovation. Explore novel model application forms; enhance complex task handling; optimize interaction. Establish comprehensive model evaluation systems.

### (VIII) Strengthen Data Supply Innovation

Prioritize building high-quality AI datasets; perfect data property rights and copyright systems; legally open publicly-financed copyright content; explore value-contribution-based data compensation and profit-sharing; incentivize data supply. Support data annotation and synthesis technologies; develop data processing and service industries.

### (IX) Reinforce Intelligent Computing Power Coordination

Support AI chip innovation and enabling software ecosystems; advance ultra-large intelligent computing cluster technologies. Optimize national computing resource distribution; perfect integrated computing networks; leverage "Eastern Data, Western Computing" hubs (东数西算); enhance resource coordination. Strengthen computing power interconnection and supply-demand matching; innovate infrastructure operations; develop standardized, scalable cloud services; ensure computing supply is equitable, efficient, green, safe.

### (X) Optimize Application Development Environment

Establish national AI application pilot testing bases; build industry-shared platforms. Transition software-information enterprises; restructure products and services. Cultivate AI application service providers; develop "model-as-service" (MaaS), "intelligent-entity-as-service" (AaaS); build application service chains. Perfect scenario construction guidance, evaluation, incentive policies; improve application error-tolerance management. Strengthen intellectual property protection; accelerate standard research; promote cross-industry, cross-domain, international standards coordination.

### (XI) Promote Open-Source Ecosystem Flourishing

Support AI open-source community development; aggregate models, tools, datasets; cultivate quality projects. Establish open-source contribution evaluation and incentive mechanisms; integrate open-source contributions into university credit systems and faculty recognition. Support enterprises, universities, research institutions exploring equitable open-source models. Construct globally-open technical systems and community ecosystems; develop internationally-influential projects and tools.

### (XII) Strengthen Talent Development

Advance K-12 through higher education and public AI literacy; perfect disciplinary structures; intensify elite talent cultivation; establish extraordinary leadership development models; strengthen faculty; promote industry-academia integration, interdisciplinary training, international cooperation. Perfect multi-dimensional evaluation systems; leverage leadership talent; provide space for young researchers; encourage exploration of AI frontiers. Support enterprise incentive mechanisms — equity, options — for talent attraction and retention.

### (XIII) Reinforce Policy and Legal Protections

Improve state capital AI investment evaluation and risk management systems. Increase AI financial and fiscal support; develop **long-term capital, patient capital, strategic capital**; perfect risk-sharing and exit mechanisms; leverage government procurement. Perfect AI legal frameworks, ethical guidelines; advance legislation. Optimize AI safety assessment and registration management.

### (XIV) Elevate Safety Capacity

Build safety capabilities across models, algorithms, data, infrastructure, applications; prevent **black-box effects, hallucinations, algorithmic bias** risks; strengthen early evaluation and response. Establish AI monitoring, risk prediction, emergency response systems; reinforce government guidance and industry discipline; adopt **"inclusive but prudent" approaches (包容审慎)**; develop dynamic, collaborative AI governance frameworks.

## IV. Implementation

Ensure Party leadership throughout "AI+" implementation. The **National Development and Reform Commission (NDRC, 国家发展改革委)** shall coordinate, building synergy. All regions and departments shall adapt implementation to local conditions, ensuring effectiveness. Strengthen demonstration and learning. Enhance communication; build broad consensus; cultivate society-wide participation.

**State Council**

**21 August 2025**

## Translator's notes on key terminology

| Chinese term | English rendering | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 人工智能+ | "AI+" | Explicit parallel to 互联网+ ("Internet+") from 2015 — signals horizontal integration across all sectors |
| 新质生产力 | "new productive forces" | Marxist political-economy term repurposed under Xi for technology-led productivity |
| 智向善 | "smart-for-good" | Ethics frame distinct from EU "trustworthy AI" / US "American values" |
| 包容审慎 | "inclusive but prudent" | Risk-tier regulatory approach, parallel to EU AI Act risk classification but with Chinese developmental-state framing |
| 长期资本、耐心资本、战略资本 | "long-term, patient, strategic capital" | State-directed investment philosophy |
| 东数西算 | "Eastern Data, Western Computing" | National compute infrastructure programme — east-coast demand, west-region supply |
| 模型即服务 | "Model-as-Service" (MaaS) | Industrial framing of foundation models as platform infrastructure |
| 智能体即服务 | "Intelligent-Entity-as-Service" (AaaS) | Industrial framing of AI agents as service layer |
